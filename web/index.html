Groth16 pairing-equation simulator

Explore a toy modular-exponentiation identity and an independent threshold-based audit. Everything runs in this browser tab without an API or Python runtime.

Not cryptographic verification. This model uses 2^(a·b) mod p, not elliptic-curve pairings, R1CS constraints, or a real Groth16 proving system. A passing result proves only that the toy arithmetic identity matches. Do not use it to authenticate proofs, verify compliance, or protect sensitive data.

Proof equation

Checks e(A,B) = e(α,β) × e(L,γ) × e(C,δ), with L = IC[0] + Σ(xᵢ · IC[i+1]) mod p.

Threshold audit demonstration

Separate, deterministic classification rules from the Python audit engine. No proof verification and no signed ledger.

Rules: primary metric > 25; secondary metric > 12 or critical flag; descriptors containing DISCORDANT, ANOMALY, MUTANT, VIOLATION, FAIL, or REJECT.

Results are generated from the entered values, never placeholders. Do not enter patient identifiers.
